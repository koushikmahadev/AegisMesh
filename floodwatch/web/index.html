ℹ️ Model & Ground-Truth Calibration ▼

Architecture: SegFormer-B0 (jointly pre-trained on drone FloodNet + fine-tuned on SpaceNet-8). Uses Mix Transformer (MiT) encoder without positional encodings.

  • Fast Mode: Whole-tile 768px resize (Warm end-to-end median: 498.1229000259191 ms, F1: 20.5%, IoU: 11.42%).
  • Accurate Mode: 512px sliding window (stride 256, chunked ≤ 8 to fit 4 GB VRAM; F1: 28.4%, IoU: 16.55%).
  • GT Composition & Recall: Roads comprise 45.37% of GT pixels and buildings 54.85%. Road recall is 4.69% (Fast) and 8.88% (Accurate); building recall is 21.27% (Fast) and 35.57% (Accurate).
  • Dataset & Evaluation Context: All 66 flooded Germany tiles were in the training pool, the training script held out no satellite tiles, and checkpoint selection used drone validation mIoU only. So pixel F1, recall, and AUC figures are computed on training tiles and are not a held-out estimate. Across 136 not-in-pool tiles evaluated to check for false alarms on unflooded terrain: In Fast Mode, 0 had post-event flooded area (class 1) > 0.1% (max: 0.02% on 0_29_68), 1 had pre-event false positive flooded area > 0.1% (3.32% on 0_20_66; cause not determined), and 134 had net exactly 0.00%. In Accurate Mode, false alarms on dry tiles increase to 8 tiles with post-event flooded area > 0.1% (max: 1.33% on 0_38_69).
  • GT Heuristic: Road vectors rasterized using a 2m metric buffer assumption (9.55 px average corridor width at 50.53°N).
