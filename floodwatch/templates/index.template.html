Architecture: SegFormer-B0 (jointly pre-trained on drone FloodNet + fine-tuned on SpaceNet-8). Uses Mix Transformer (MiT) encoder without positional encodings.

  • Fast Mode: Whole-tile 768px resize (Warm end-to-end median: {{measured.benchmarks.warm_end_to_end_uncached_stage1.median_ms}} ms, F1: {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.fast_768.f1_pct}}%, IoU: {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.fast_768.iou_pct}}%).
  • Accurate Mode: 512px sliding window (stride 256, chunked ≤ 8 to fit 4 GB VRAM; F1: {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.accurate_512_sliding.f1_pct}}%, IoU: {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.accurate_512_sliding.iou_pct}}%).
  • GT Composition & Recall: Roads comprise {{measured.dataset.gt_composition_2m.road_pct}}% of GT pixels and buildings {{measured.dataset.gt_composition_2m.building_pct}}%. Road recall is {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.fast_768.road_recall_pct}}% (Fast) and {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.accurate_512_sliding.road_recall_pct}}% (Accurate); building recall is {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.fast_768.building_recall_pct}}% (Fast) and {{measured.dataset.accuracy_modes_2m.accurate_512_sliding.building_recall_pct}}% (Accurate).
  • Dataset & Evaluation Context: All {{measured.dataset.training_pool_tiles}} flooded Germany tiles were in the training pool, the training script held out no satellite tiles, and checkpoint selection used drone validation mIoU only. So pixel F1, recall, and AUC figures are computed on training tiles and are not a held-out estimate. Across {{measured.dataset.non_training_tiles}} not-in-pool tiles evaluated to check for false alarms on unflooded terrain: In Fast Mode, 0 had post-event flooded area (class 1) > 0.1% (max: {{measured.dataset.dry_tiles_false_alarm_comparison_136.fast_mode.max_after}}% on 0_29_68), 1 had pre-event false positive flooded area > 0.1% ({{measured.dataset.dry_tiles_false_alarm_comparison_136.fast_mode.max_before}}% on 0_20_66; cause not determined), and {{measured.dataset.dry_tiles_false_alarm_comparison_136.fast_mode.count_net_eq_0}} had net exactly 0.00%. In Accurate Mode, false alarms on dry tiles increase to {{measured.dataset.dry_tiles_false_alarm_comparison_136.accurate_mode.count_after_gt_0_1}} tiles with post-event flooded area > 0.1% (max: {{measured.dataset.dry_tiles_false_alarm_comparison_136.accurate_mode.max_after}}% on 0_38_69).
